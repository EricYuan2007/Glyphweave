# Code of Conduct

Glyphweave welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful,
assume good intent, and discuss technical ideas with evidence. Accept constructive
feedback and acknowledge mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, and publication of another person's private information are not allowed.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and based on
the conduct involved. Participants may request a review of a moderation decision.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action for abuse or sensitive concerns. For a
non-sensitive moderation question, contact the repository maintainer through a
project issue without reproducing private information. Do not publish private
incident details in an issue.
